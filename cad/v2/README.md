# Samsung Stand v2

Validated second structural increment built on the published v1 STEP baseline.

Added:
- annular-sector glide tracks on the fixed left/right base modules;
- track top coplanar with the installed rotor top;
- two form-locking rotor receivers;
- one universal INNER_ARM printed twice;
- replaceable transverse PETG snap-lock pin;
- replaceable saddle cartridge.

Load-path intent:
- central load enters the rotor through its broad annular support;
- each inner arm is supported at its root by the rotor and near the real load station by its own side glide track;
- the inner arm is therefore not treated as a long cantilever from the center.

Important unresolved manufacturing input:
- the exact Samsung stand-arm cross-section is not inferred into the structural part.
  samsung_stand_v2_saddle_insert_blank is intentionally only a calibration blank.
  The final contact insert will be generated after physical cross-section measurements.

OUTER_GUIDE parts and final swivel detents/end-stops are not part of v2 yet.
