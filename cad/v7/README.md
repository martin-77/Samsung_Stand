# Samsung Stand v7

v7 adds replaceable flush PETG wear surfaces without changing the validated v5 geometry planes.

Added service parts:
- 1x center wear ring, 1.2 mm thick;
- 2x universal side-track wear arcs, 1.2 mm thick.

The fixed base receives shallow recesses. Installed wear pieces restore:
- center bearing top to the original z=10 mm plane;
- side glide-track top to the original z=18 mm plane.

The rotor, INNER_ARM, OUTER_GUIDE, end stops and detent geometry remain at their v5 positions.

Purpose:
normal PETG-on-PETG sliding wear is moved onto cheap replaceable parts rather than the large structural modules.

This remains a geometry candidate until the physical release gates are completed.
