# Samsung Stand v8

v8 replaces the previously unproven retainer concepts with explicitly assembled and OCC-validated PETG retention.

Changes:
- 4 horizontal snap pins lock BASE_LEFT/RIGHT to BASE_CENTER entirely above the Sounddeck plane;
- 2 compact snap pins lock ROTOR to the two INNER_ARM parts;
- 2 longer snap pins pass completely through the INNER_ARM saddle region and lock the OUTER_GUIDE parts from an externally accessible side;
- the fixed pivot post is extended and cross-drilled with a support-free roof tunnel;
- 1 pivot cross-pin sits inside a circular rotor counterbore and positively limits axial lift while preserving rotation.

Removed from the v8 print list:
- legacy vertical base retainers;
- legacy pivot C-clip;
- legacy v2 arm lock pin.

The v7 replaceable wear surfaces and v5 detent/end-stop architecture remain upstream.

Geometry validation is not physical structural release. Fit/proof/cycling gates in docs/PHYSICAL_RELEASE_GATES.md still apply.
