# Samsung Stand v5

v5 adds a replaceable zero-position detent calibration cassette to the validated v4 structure.

Architecture:
- only 0 degrees is detented;
- +/-15 degrees remain positive structural end stops from v4;
- the rotor carries a flat cam sector with a shallow V-notch at zero;
- a fixed, replaceable PETG cantilever cassette engages that track;
- the cassette is printed flat so the spring bends in the XY plane;
- two removable locating pins retain the cassette on a fixed platform.

Calibration variants:
- t18: 1.8 mm spring thickness;
- t22: 2.2 mm spring thickness;
- t26: 2.6 mm spring thickness.

The beam strain estimates are geometric only. The documented force ranges use a broad nominal PETG modulus range and are not treated as measured print behavior. Print one calibration variant first before choosing the final detent feel.

v5 re-validates the v4 mechanical end stops after adding the cam and fixed mount.
